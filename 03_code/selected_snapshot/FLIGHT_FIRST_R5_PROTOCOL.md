# r5 flight-first refinement: frozen development protocol

Date: 20 September 2026. Authorised action: refine FAQ extraction and flight-first ordering, then generate. Historical release r4 and its completed answers remain unchanged.

Content intervention: on flight pages, convert only already-expanded question-shaped AccordionTitle buttons within an h3 and a single paired AccordionContent container to noninteractive spans. Preserve question wording, answer HTML, links and conditions. Move the entire FAQ section before an earlier Explore destination section where both have unambiguous, separate direct-main containers. Other buttons remain untouched. Do not generate new facts, new questions or summary text. JSON-LD remains unchanged. Unsupported structures are recorded as no-ops or rejected if ambiguous. The original cleaner, chunker, retrieval pipeline, competitor pages, source ordering and context budgets remain unchanged.

The content transform does not read queries, outcomes or retrieval ranks. Its design was informed by previously inspected outcomes. Consequently this is exploratory development research, not confirmation on unseen queries. The 60% fixed citation target is aspirational; it is not an early stopping rule or a release condition.

Three fresh conditions: baseline_repeat, v3_final_core (sealed r4), v3_final_flight_first_r5. Both fixed and reindexed layers. Reuse the original 800-job schedule per condition (30 fixed queries and 50 reindexed queries, two context-order seeds and five responses per seed). Total: 2,400 answers. All query strata retained; report primary subsets separately. Schedule matched triplets with a declared random seed, rotating uniformly among the six within-triplet orders. Four concurrent triplet workers; requests within a triplet are sequential. Scheduling seeds do not seed model generation. Use the locked baseline generator and provider defaults, preserving response provenance.

Retain the baseline's declared primary endpoint, reindexed/primary/citation_inclusion. The refinement's decision focus is the incremental r5-minus-r4 fixed/primary citation-inclusion effect, explicitly secondary and exploratory. Report r4-minus-baseline and r5-minus-baseline too, and every comparison for both layers. Fixed primary has 29 queries and 290 answers per condition; reindexed primary has 40 and 400. Report paired query-bootstrap 95% intervals, with 5,000 resamples and fixed seed 20260908. These are unadjusted exploratory intervals, not familywise confirmatory tests. Keep retrieval inclusion and citation share secondary. Do not claim benefits based on a favourable subset, an unplanned stopping time, or summing component effects.

Prepare once and freeze prompts, pages, vectors, manifests and schedule before answering. Reuse exact matching historical vectors only from validated packages using the same embedding model. Save every new embedding batch with its input/output hashes and provenance, allowing a failed preparation to resume without recapturing or regenerating content. Reproduce baseline and r4 retrieval contexts and fixed prompts before accepting the new package. No fresh model answers are copied from prior runs. Competitors must remain byte-identical in the evaluated text.

On provider failure, stop bounded submission and retain completed records. Resume only missing records under the same lock; record resumption timing. Run the complete fixed budget once; do not stop when a threshold is reached. A future genuinely unseen query set and citation-support audit are required before treating any result as confirmed or making deployment claims.

Known limitation: preserving complete FAQ units in HTML does not guarantee each entire unit fits a fixed-size reindexed passage. Report such boundary cases without changing the common chunker. Historical content may contain dated or unverified original claims; the transformation certifies preservation and provenance, not their real-world truth.

Research motivation: GEO (KDD 2024), Lost in the Middle (TACL 2024), C-SEO Bench (NeurIPS 2025), and ALCE (EMNLP 2023), discussed with limits in FIXED_VISIBILITY_60_PERCENT_RESEARCH_2026-09-20.md. No cited work establishes a guaranteed gain for this implementation.
