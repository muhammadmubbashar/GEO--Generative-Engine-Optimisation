10. How we checked the page changes

Before trusting the comparison, we checked three things. First, were we using the correct saved versions? Second, did the final question-restoration change preserve the intended information? Third, did the text sent to the AI match the saved output? We rebuilt the final FAQ stage and matched its saved page outputs. We also checked the Flight Centre text recorded in the selected model inputs against those outputs. This catches mistakes such as displaying one page but testing another. The check has a boundary: it verifies that the implementation did what we intended, not that every inherited travel statement is correct or that a public AI service will behave the same way.
