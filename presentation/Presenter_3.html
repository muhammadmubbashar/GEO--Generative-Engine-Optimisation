8. Offer information before and after

This is a real archived representation from a Melbourne-to-London page. The baseline opening contains a headline fare and fee information. The rendered offer record contains a carrier, economy return trip type, date range and fare. The purpose is to expose useful product information in a form the model can read. Notice that the prices differ. We cannot claim a like-for-like fare comparison here: the headline and rendered card have different capture and product contexts. The selected experimental package retained these original rendered offers. This is a confound we disclose, not an improvement in pricing. A later price-matched treatment is a different version and cannot be used as the evidence behind the fifty-two-point-four-percent result.

9. Restoring a captured FAQ question

This example shows a more tightly controlled change. The page contained a question inside an accordion button. Our common cleaner removed interactive buttons, so the question label could disappear even when its expanded answer remained. The refinement converts an eligible captured question label into readable text and keeps it next to the same answer. It does not invent an answer. It only handles clearly paired, already-expanded sections. The before-and-after view shows why an answer without its question can be less useful, and how restoring that relationship improves the representation sent to the model.
