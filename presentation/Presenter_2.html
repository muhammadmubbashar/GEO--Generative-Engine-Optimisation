4. A matched comparison of two reading packs

Three search variants were merged into saved candidate pools. Of forty primary questions, twenty-nine had a Flight Centre page in the first-ten analysis pool and were eligible for this comparison. We then prepared two reading packs for each question: the baseline and the treatment. The source identities, source order, competitor texts and instruction stayed matched. Only the Flight Centre representation changed. Two source arrangements and five repeats produced ten observations per question, or two hundred and ninety per condition. We verified those matches directly in the saved records. The baseline answers are historical, rather than freshly generated alongside the treatment. This limits causal certainty. The result also says nothing directly about the eleven questions where FC was outside the fixed analysis pool.

5. Individual methods: results and interpretation

These six method families were explored separately. Rendered content exposes captured offers and semantic cleanup keeps labels and digits intelligible. Grounded FAQ pairs supported questions with answers. Structured facts organise existing information into fields. These three are intended to improve how the model reads information. External enrichment adds attributed outside material; schema adds structure; rewriting changes the wording more broadly. All six rows here use the same thirty-nine-point-three-percent historical control. FAQ and typed facts have positive exploratory intervals; rendering’s interval crosses zero. External enrichment does not show a clear isolated gain. The schema experiment also changed visible text, so its improvement cannot be assigned to JSON-LD. Automatic rewriting declined. These are unadjusted exploratory comparisons across multiple versions, not a definitive ranking or six proven ingredients.

6. Selection of the combined treatment

We did not combine every method or add their percentage gains together. The selected package uses captured rendered content, semantic cleanup, joint grounded facts and questions, and a final FAQ extraction refinement. Added external enrichment, added schema, broad rewriting and source positioning remain separate evidence. The choice considers implementation and compatibility as well as scores. The typed-facts variant in the preceding table is not identical to every fact component later used in the package. The combined version must therefore be tested as its own treatment. We cannot assign a particular share of its improvement to each component.
