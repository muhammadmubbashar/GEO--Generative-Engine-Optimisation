13. Question-level uncertainty and variation

Repeated answers to one question are related, so we analyse uncertainty by resampling whole questions while keeping their ten observations together. In the independently recalculated analysis, the initial gain has a ninety-five-percent interval from about three-point-one to fifteen-point-nine percentage points. The repeat gain has an interval from about five-point-two to twenty. Both are positive in these saved comparisons. However, the difference between the two treatment runs has an interval crossing zero. We should not claim the repeat is better. The repeat also improves twenty-one question averages, declines on six and ties on two. These intervals do not correct for trying many versions or using a historical control. Our conclusion remains exploratory.

14. Source-order sensitivity

We also checked source ordering. In the corrected position experiment, source text stayed the same, the first Flight Centre source moved to the beginning, and competitor relative order was preserved. The rate moved from thirty-nine-point-three to fifty-seven-point-six percent. This suggests our measured visibility is sensitive to where evidence appears inside the model’s reading pack. It is consistent with research showing that models can use context differently depending on position. It does not show that Flight Centre can control a public engine’s internal source order, and position manipulation was not included in the final combined page package.

15. Practical value for Flight Centre

The practical output is a working content-transformation prototype and a way to test its effect. Flight Centre could use the approach to make existing route information, conditions and useful answers easier to extract, while maintaining a governed product source. The business value demonstrated here is a better opportunity for source credit when that information is already in the assistant’s reading pack. We have not demonstrated more booking recommendations or sales. A live application would need current prices, content ownership and separate measurement of discovery, supported recommendations and customer actions. Those are proposed applications and evaluation steps, not additional achievements we are claiming for this semester.
