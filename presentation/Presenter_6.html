16. What this experiment cannot prove

We want the conclusion to be useful without overstating it. The main test supplied the same sources to both conditions; it does not represent every step of a public AI search. In a separate test where retrieval exposure could change, we did not establish a gain. Fresh rewordings also gave a less certain result. Our comparison uses earlier baseline answers and captures from different contexts, and development was informed by earlier results. These facts limit causal certainty and generalisation. A citation also needs a separate human check to establish whether it supports the statement. These limitations do not erase the prototype or observed result; they define what the evidence can support.

18. Our conclusion

We delivered a working experiment and a combined page-improvement prototype. The selected pages were cited more often than the historical baseline in both saved treatment runs. The strongest conclusion is that clearer page representation can improve source use when the information is already supplied to the model. We have not established more booking recommendations or sales. Our submission preserves the implementation, real examples, original outputs and calculation steps.
